# Scope and verification limits

Accepted-transfer semantics, packing, framing, buffering, reset and clock-domain boundaries in streaming hardware.

The supplied monitor and checker target one-pixel, full-keep progressive video. Simulation and protocol checks do not establish implemented timing, metastability safety or physical losslessness.

Use the checks appropriate to the requested change and report which were actually executed. Package contents are reusable procedures and helpers; they do not certify a new environment or target.
