# Scope and verification limits

Comparable performance metrics, evidence-based bottleneck diagnosis and bounded optimization decisions.

Build success, a local stage estimate and unrelated historical measurements cannot establish end-to-end speedup. Compare matching workloads and retain correctness, loss and latency evidence.

Use the checks appropriate to the requested change and report which were actually executed. Package contents are reusable procedures and helpers; they do not certify a new environment or target.
