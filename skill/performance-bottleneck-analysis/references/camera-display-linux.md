# Camera, display and Linux measurement extensions

Distinguish sensor SOF, accepted pixels, DMA completion notifications, unique source frames, processed frames, decoded instances, business publications and display scanout. Verify what each counter actually counts. Refresh rate and repeated-image transport do not prove new optical input.

For optical accuracy, bind physical target instances and eligibility windows to synchronized truth, camera ROI, scene/seed, focus, viewport and capture settings. Unique payloads are not a denominator; identical payloads can represent separate physical passes. Report boundary targets and late arrivals by the declared rule. Changed focus/framing invalidates an unchanged-scene comparison.

For HDMI/output, distinguish render/queue submission, display acknowledgement where available and physical observation. A controller heartbeat proves liveness, not correct focus, page, optics or visible scene. Preserve human observations and current authorization separately from historical reports.

For Linux, identify the actual process/library/driver and running configuration. Inspect relevant CPU/I/O/queue scheduling, allocator/backend and ownership behavior without defaulting to kernel or hardware replacement. An imported package or metadata parse does not prove allocation, DMA or display capability.

Use same-input replay to isolate processing changes, and actual target execution for target-specific performance. Neither replay nor a native host executable establishes optics, device I/O timing or another architecture's throughput. A full image rebuild is justified by changed dependencies, not by a general performance request.
